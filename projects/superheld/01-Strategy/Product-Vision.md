# Product Vision

## Positioning

Superheld should be framed as a personal security platform for households:

- protect devices
- protect accounts and identity
- guide people through incidents with clear, safe actions

This is stronger and more honest than positioning it as "antivirus for consumers".

## Who It Is For

- families and households with children
- parents or guardians who need shared visibility and approvals
- security-aware individuals who want guided support around scams, spam, and account risk

## North Star

When a user is at risk, Superheld should reduce confusion and compress time-to-safe-action.

## Product Principles

- one incident should lead to one clear user action
- rules and playbooks must be auditable
- privacy is part of the product, not a later layer
- risky remediations require approval
- explanation quality matters as much as detection quality
- learning should be built into the product, not treated as a separate content project
