# Family Safety Pillars

## Product Pillars

Superheld should now be planned around five core pillars:

1. family and child digital safety
2. social media and messenger protection
3. fake, impersonation, and deepfake defense
4. spam, scam, and phishing support
5. learning center for parents and children

## 1. Family and Child Digital Safety

- household-level visibility
- parent or guardian approvals
- age-appropriate guidance
- shared incident understanding across the family

## 2. Social Media and Messenger Protection

- help assess suspicious direct messages, links, and contact requests
- support reporting, blocking, and safe next actions
- work as a companion layer around platforms rather than pretending to fully control them

## 3. Fake, Impersonation, and Deepfake Defense

- help households review fake profiles, fake account warnings, and manipulated media
- support authenticity checks for suspicious image, video, audio, or call scenarios
- focus on evidence preservation, safe reporting, and escalation rather than claiming perfect detection

## 4. Spam, Scam, and Phishing Support

- identify suspicious patterns
- explain risk clearly
- guide the user through safe actions
- escalate to account-recovery support when needed

## 5. Learning Center

- short, age-appropriate modules
- parent and child variants where useful
- connect lessons to real incidents and recurring risk themes
- focus on action, not generic theory

## Planning Implication

These pillars move Superheld closer to a family digital trust and safety platform than a deep endpoint-first security product.
