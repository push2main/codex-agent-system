# MVP Scope

## Recommended Must-Haves

- family safety dashboard and household approvals
- social media and messenger risk guidance
- fake profile, impersonation, and deepfake review support
- spam, scam, and phishing detection support
- learning center for parents and children
- phishing, scam link, and fake account warning response
- infostealer-style account recovery guidance

## Recommended Platform Scope

- Web: family dashboard, approvals, and learning center
- Android: companion and social-risk workflows
- iOS: companion and social-risk workflows
- Desktop: later advanced protection track

## What To Delay

- deep Linux support
- full antivirus positioning
- autonomous high-risk remediation
- broad B2B or SOC workflows
- advanced ML-first detection

## MVP Success Criteria

- detect a small set of family-relevant risks well
- explain why the incident matters in consumer language for both adults and children where appropriate
- give a safe next action within minutes
- connect core incidents to useful learning content
- keep telemetry intentionally minimal

## Decision Needed From Product

Confirm the 3-5 protection cases that are mandatory for launch. The current recommendation is:

1. suspicious social messages and spam or scam contact
2. fake account warnings and scam links
3. fake profiles, impersonation, and deepfake risk
4. infostealer fallout and account recovery
5. household approvals and learning recommendations tied to family risk
