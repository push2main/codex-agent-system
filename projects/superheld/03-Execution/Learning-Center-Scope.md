# Learning Center Scope

## Purpose

The Learning Center should turn recurring family risk into short, practical learning moments.

## Target Users

- parents or guardians
- children and teenagers
- household members who need simple, actionable explanations

## Initial Module Types

- suspicious messages, fake profiles, and social-media scams
- phishing links, fake login pages, and fake account warnings
- deepfakes, manipulated media, and cyberbullying basics
- password and MFA hygiene
- privacy, evidence preservation, and reporting basics on major social platforms

## Product Rules

- keep modules short
- use age-appropriate language
- attach learning suggestions to real incidents where possible
- prefer concrete actions over generic awareness content
- distinguish parent-facing and child-facing guidance when the same incident needs both

## MVP Shape

- incident-driven recommendations
- a simple module library
- completion tracking for household members
- parent-friendly summaries when a child-facing module is recommended
- short authenticity and anti-scam checklists that can be reused across incidents

## Out of Scope for the First Cycle

- gamified world-building
- broad certification-style courses
- long-form content that is detached from product incidents
- a universal deepfake detector curriculum that promises certainty
