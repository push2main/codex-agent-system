# C4 Fraud Scenario Map

## Purpose

This note maps public Austrian C4, BMI, and onlinesicherheit.gv.at fraud scenarios into a practical family-safety scope for Superheld.

## Date Context

Planning baseline checked on 2026-03-29.

## Official Scenario Clusters

### 1. Deepfake authority and emergency payment fraud

- fake video or audio calls that imitate trusted officials or support staff
- spoofed phone numbers and urgent money requests
- strong relevance for parent-facing scam guidance and household approvals

### 2. Fake profiles, celebrity clones, and impersonation

- fake social profiles that imitate celebrities, brands, or trusted contacts
- romance, trust-building, or off-platform contact lures
- strong relevance for suspicious DM review, fake-profile risk, and report guidance

### 3. Fake account warnings and takeover setup

- fake Meta, Facebook, Instagram, or other platform messages about account suspension
- phishing-style follow-up to harvest credentials or MFA codes
- strong relevance for link review, account recovery, and learning modules

### 4. Fake prize notices, social ads, and ad-driven scams

- hijacked giveaways, fake shops, fake jobs, and investment scams
- strong relevance for family-safe explanation and quick link or profile review

### 5. Deepfake cyberbullying and manipulated-media abuse

- manipulated images, videos, or audio used for humiliation, coercion, or extortion
- strong relevance for children, teenagers, evidence preservation, and reporting guidance

## Product Implications For Superheld

- Superheld should treat authenticity support as bounded review and guided response, not as a promise of perfect deepfake detection.
- The first product value comes from explanation, evidence preservation, reporting guidance, approvals, and learning.
- Family-safe handling needs parent-facing and child-facing variants for the same incident where appropriate.
- Social-platform protection should be companion-style, because first-party control over third-party platforms is limited.

## Recommended First Five MVP Scenarios

1. suspicious DM from an unknown or fake profile
2. fake account warning or fake platform-support message
3. fake giveaway, fake shop, or ad-driven social scam
4. manipulated image, video, or audio used for bullying, extortion, or impersonation
5. account recovery after a scam or phishing step

## Detection Boundaries

- prioritize user-shared content, manual reporting, and companion-style review
- support simple heuristics, deterministic rules, and evidence-based incident creation
- avoid claiming universal or automated deepfake truth detection across the internet

## Learning Center Implications

- how to spot fake profiles and impersonation
- how to react to fake account warnings
- how to recognize giveaway, prize, and ad scams
- what deepfakes are and what to do when media feels manipulated
- how to preserve evidence and report safely
